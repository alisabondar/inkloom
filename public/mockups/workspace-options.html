Background accents: pick one number

What sits behind the sheet on the desk, replacing the watercolour blob. Every option uses the template's own palette colours (ochre, sage, sky here). All are flat vector shapes: no turbulence filter, no splat mask.

1Nothing

Desk grain and the sheet's shadow only. The artwork is the only colour.

2Soft circles

Two large flat circles at low opacity, partly under the sheet.

3Colour-chart swatches

Square paint swatches with a white name band, like a hand-painted colour chart.

4One brushstroke

A single flat swipe behind the sheet, slightly irregular ends, no texture.

5Palette strip

A neat row of colour bars along the bottom edge of the desk, like a paint-chip strip.

6Paper cut-outs

Hard-edged organic paper shapes in palette colours, like Matisse cut-outs. Crisp, not blobby.

7Washi tape

Colour comes only from the tape holding the sheet down, tinted with palette colours.

8Coloured shadow

No shapes at all; the sheet casts a soft shadow tinted with the dominant palette colour.
