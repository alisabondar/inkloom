The blend: Studio Table workspace + Wet Paint accents

The canvas is a sheet of cold-press paper taped to the desk, lit by late-afternoon window light. Watercolour from the concept's palette has bled out from under the sheet, with irregular edges, darker dried rims and granulation. The paint tin is the palette, the layers are tracing-paper sheets, and the sliders are pencil lines with paint-dab handles. The paper in the middle stays clean and calm.
