C · Serif-led

Slider handles (replacing the splat)

Same track, four handle shapes. All are crisp vector shapes in a palette colour; none use the splat mask.

Dot

A round paint dab with a paper ring. Quiet, familiar, works at every size.

Ring

Outlined circle like most design tools. Most neutral; the fill colour shows on the track only.

Paint swatch

A tiny paint chip with a white label band. Ties sliders to the palette idea.

Pencil tip

A small pointer on a dashed pencil line. Most "drawing"-flavoured, slightly harder to grab.

Background accents (replacing the watercolour blobs)

What sits behind the sheets. Each uses the template's own palette colours.

None

Just the desk grain and paper shadows. Cleanest; the art is the only colour.

Soft circles

Two large flat circles at low opacity. Colourful without texture or wobble.

Paint swatches

Labelled colour chips from the palette, tucked under the sheet like paint samples.

Brushstroke

One flat, slightly irregular swipe per colour. Painterly but clean-edged.
